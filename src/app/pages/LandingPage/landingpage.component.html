What is FaB Tier List

This application aggregates data from Talishar and presents monthly meta trends in a structured, tier-based format. Its purpose is exclusively educational and aimed at supporting the community.

Things to Know

As the data from Talishar is entered manually, this site will be updated biweekly with the latest information. If heroes do not appear in the tiers immediately, please allow up to a minute for the service to resume from idle.

Nerd stuff (How Tiers are calculated)

Each month, the algorithm aggregates the total number of games played across all heroes, filtering out those with high win rates but insufficient play volume. It then calculates the mean and standard deviation for eligible decks. If a deck exceeds 1.5 standard deviations above the mean, an S tier is created. Tiers are then assigned as follows: S – top 5%, A – next 15%, B and C – next 25%, and D – all remaining decks.

The Future

A feature will be added to allow users to select a hero and view tiered matchups for the current month. For example, selecting Slippy in January 2026 would display the decks that hero is favored or unfavored against, organized into tiers.
